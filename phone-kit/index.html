When you close, the phone still rings

After the shop is closed, people still call. On most lines, those calls ring a few times and then go to voicemail. Some callers leave a message. Some hang up.

This kit has two things you can use on your own, tonight:

  1. Call-forwarding codes published by the major U.S. carriers, so unanswered or busy calls can go to another number you choose (a cell, a partner, or another line).
  2. Short after-hours voicemail greetings you can copy and record, written for six common trades.

If you’d rather skip voicemail altogether, Sarah can answer those calls for you. That’s at the bottom, with a number you can call to hear her.

Forward the calls you don’t answer

Dial these from the phone line you want to forward — usually the business number itself. Replace NUMBER with the 10-digit U.S. number the calls should go to, including the area code, with no spaces or dashes unless the carrier’s example uses them.

Listen for the confirmation tone or message, then test: call the business number from a different phone and let it ring (or, if you set “all calls,” it should forward right away).

Two kinds of forwarding

  • No-answer / busy (conditional): your phone still rings first. If you don’t pick up, or the line is busy, the call goes to the other number instead of voicemail. This is the usual after-hours setup if you still want to catch calls when you’re free.
  • All calls (unconditional): every inbound call goes straight to the other number. Your phone will not ring. Use this when you’re closed and don’t want the business line to ring at all.

Codes can vary by plan and by area. If a code doesn’t work, use the carrier’s help page in Sources, or ask the carrier. Features are not on every plan.

Google Voice and RingCentral

These services do not publish star codes for no-answer or busy forwarding. You set forwarding in the app or a web portal.

Google Voice

  1. On a computer, go to voice.google.com (or use the Voice app).
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Link the phone number that should receive calls: Account → Linked numbers → New linked number, then verify the number. Google says you can link up to six numbers, and that a number already linked to another Google Voice number can’t be added.
  4. Under Settings → Calls, use Incoming calls and Call forwarding to choose which of your devices and linked numbers should ring.
  5. To stop forwarding to a linked number, turn that number off under Call forwarding. Google notes that turning forwarding off does not remove the linked number. To remove it, go to Devices and numbers and tap Remove.

Google’s official help also says: “Forwarding calls from your Google Voice number to an automated system is unsupported.” If you want those calls to go to an auto attendant or an AI receptionist, check with Google, or use a different line.

Workspace accounts can send calls to voicemail after working hours by following Google Calendar hours under Do not disturb. That is voicemail, not forwarding to another number.

RingCentral (RingEX app or Admin Portal)

  1. In the RingCentral desktop or web app, go to Settings → Phone.
  2. Scroll to Call handling and click Edit.
  3. Set Work hours and After hours. If work hours are set to 24 hours, 7 days a week, after-hours uses the same settings; click Edit Schedule to set closing hours.
  4. Under Missed calls, choose Forward to external number and enter the 10-digit number. Other official options are send to voicemail, play a message and disconnect, or forward to an extension.
  5. To send every call, use Forward all calls (from your profile / call rules) and enter an extension or external number. You can add a schedule, or turn it on and off by hand.
  6. Click Save. To undo missed-call forwarding, edit Call handling again and change Missed calls back to voicemail (or your previous setting). Turn Forward all calls off to take calls on the RingCentral line again.

After-hours voicemail greetings

Copy a greeting, replace [Business Name], and record it on your voicemail. Keep it short. Say the name clearly, ask for the basics, and stop.

Plumbing

Hi, you’ve reached [Business Name]. We’re closed right now. Please leave your name, number, address, and a short description of the plumbing issue, and we’ll call you back.

HVAC

Hi, you’ve reached [Business Name]. The shop is closed at the moment. Please leave your name, number, and a short note about the heating or cooling issue, and we’ll get back to you.

Cleaning

Hi, you’ve reached [Business Name]. We’re done for the day. Please leave your name, number, and whether you’d like to book a cleaning or change an existing appointment, and we’ll call you back.

Landscaping

Hi, you’ve reached [Business Name]. We’re away from the phone right now. Please leave your name, number, and a short note about the work you need, and we’ll get back to you.

Salon or barber

Hi, you’ve reached [Business Name]. We’re closed at the moment. Please leave your name, number, and the service or time you’re hoping for, and we’ll get back to you to book it.

Auto repair

Hi, you’ve reached [Business Name]. The shop is closed right now. Please leave your name, number, your vehicle, and a short description of the problem, and we’ll call you back.
